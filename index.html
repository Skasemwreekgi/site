From the amber coast to the high passes, each province sends its banners to the capital — and finds the gates already open in welcome.

The shining harbour where merchant ships first see the gate-light across the bay.

Province II

The Long Chronicle

How the gates came to open

Year 1

The First Stone

A wandering people lay the foundation of the keep upon the golden hill, and name it Aurelmark.

Year 312

The Walls Rise

The great curtain wall and twin towers are completed, and the empire knows a century of quiet strength.

Year 700

The Closed Age

Fearful of the world beyond, the gates are barred. For three hundred years no stranger crosses the threshold.

Year 1024

The Gates Thrown Wide

By decree of the Open Court the doors are unbarred forever. Aurelmark becomes the empire of all who would enter.
